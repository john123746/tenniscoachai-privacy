Last updated: 2 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TennisCoach AI ("TennisCoach AI", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects personal data when you use our mobile application and website (the "Service"). It applies to users in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area, and other jurisdictions with similar data protection laws.

1. Data Controller

TennisCoach AI is the data controller responsible for your personal data. Contact us at:
support.tenniscoachai@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Account Data

  • Email address
  • Password (hashed and salted via authentication provider)
  • Authentication method (e.g. Google Sign-In)

Profile Data

  • Name (optional)
  • Age (optional)
  • Skill level, goals, playing style
  • Strengths, weaknesses, preferences

Coaching Data

  • AI-generated coaching plans
  • Training history and progress
  • Logged activities and stats

AI Usage Data

  • User inputs to AI features
  • AI-generated outputs
  • Usage logs for abuse prevention and rate limiting

AI data is processed only to provide coaching features and is not used to train third-party general models.

Technical Data

  • IP address
  • Device and browser information
  • Operating system
  • Security and audit logs

Billing Data

  • Stripe customer ID (web)
  • Google Play purchase tokens (Android)
  • Subscription status and plan type

We do not store full payment card details.

3. Legal Basis (UK GDPR)

  • Contract: provide core app functionality
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention, improvement

4. AI Processing

We use third-party AI providers (e.g. OpenAI, Google Gemini) to generate coaching responses. Inputs are only used to generate responses and are not sold or used for advertising.

5. International Transfers

Some providers are located outside the UK/EEA. Where data is transferred internationally, we use safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and UK IDTA.

6. Payments

  • Web: Stripe processes payments
  • Android: Google Play Billing

We do not store full card details. Subscriptions must be cancelled via the original platform.

7. Security

We use encryption, hashed passwords, access controls, and audit logs. No system is 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active. Deleted accounts are removed within 30 days, except where legally required to retain data.
