TennisCoach AI – Account Deletion Request

This page explains how to request deletion of your TennisCoach AI account and associated personal data. TennisCoach AI is the data controller responsible for processing your request.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account, please email:

support.tenniscoachai@gmail.com

Please include:

  • The subject line: “Account Deletion Request”
  • The email address linked to your TennisCoach AI account

For security reasons, we may need to verify ownership of the account before processing your request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Once your request is verified, we will delete:

  • Your account and authentication data
  • Your profile information
  • Your coaching plans, drills, and saved content
  • Your practice history and progress data
  • Your preferences and in-app settings

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel active subscriptions. You must cancel subscriptions separately via:

  • Google Play (Android subscriptions)
  • Stripe billing portal (web subscriptions)

What Data May Be Retained

We may retain certain information where required by law or legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Billing and transaction records (for tax and accounting purposes)
  • Limited security and audit logs
  • Data stored in backups until they are overwritten

Deletion Timeline

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 30 days after verification. Some data may be removed earlier, while legally required records may be retained longer.

Contact

If you have questions about your request, contact:
support.tenniscoachai@gmail.com
